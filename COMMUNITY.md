> ⚠️ The projects below are maintained independently by their authors, have not been audited by this repository, and listing does not imply endorsement. Judge for yourself before using them — pay particular attention to the instructions and scripts inside a skill.

## Getting your project listed

Open a PR adding one line to the matching table. The bar is in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md):

- Person skills: your own repo + self-contained raw research + honest limits + [fidelity scorecard](references/fidelity-scorecard.md) grade B or above
- Collections, tools, and orchestration projects: a README that explains the relationship to Nuwa, and no ethics red lines

Line format: `| [owner/repo](link) | one-sentence description |`
