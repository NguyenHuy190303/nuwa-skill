# Sun Yuchen (Justin Sun) - Full Timeline

## I. Upbringing and education (1990-2014)

**July 30, 1990** — Born in Xining, Qinghai, China. Moved with his family to Huizhou, Guangdong at age 4. According to media reports, family upheaval in his teens (his mother remarrying abroad, a strained father-son relationship) led him into video-game addiction and skipping school, and his grades fell below third-tier-university admission level.

**2007** — After studying the winning formulas of past years' entries, he entered the "New Concept Composition Contest" and won first prize. This became the key springboard into Peking University.

**2007-2008** — Sat the college entrance exam under his Qinghai household registration (dubbed a "gaokao migrant" by outside observers), scoring 650 — 3 points below Peking University's cutline — but was admitted to **Peking University's History Department** on the strength of his New Concept first prize plus independent-admission bonus points. (Sources: China News Service, Sohu Education, Sina Education)

**2008-2011** — Undergraduate in Peking University's History Department. By his own account, through frequent interaction with professors and submitting papers ahead of deadlines, he graduated with the top GPA in the department. In 2011 he appeared on the cover of *Yazhou Zhoukan* (Asiaweek).

**2011-2013** — Went to the **University of Pennsylvania** for a Master of Arts in East Asian Studies. While at Penn he was introduced to Bitcoin and began investing in cryptocurrency.

**2013** — Joined **Ripple Labs** as chief representative and advisor for Greater China. This was his entry point into the blockchain industry.

**2014** — Named a "Global Shaper" by the World Economic Forum (Davos). That same year he returned to China to found **Ripple China** (a China-facing affiliate project of Ripple), working in cross-border bank clearing and settlement.

## II. Early ventures and the "Jack Ma protégé" narrative (2015-2016)

**Early 2015** — Ripple China raised a **tens-of-millions-of-dollars Series A round**, led by CMC Capital with IDG Capital and others participating.

**2015** — Admitted as part of the first cohort of Hupan University (founded by Jack Ma), the only student in the cohort born in the 1990s. This credential was later heavily used in his marketing narrative and became the source of the "Jack Ma protégé" label. That same year he was named to **Forbes China's 30 Under 30**.

**2015** — Launched the **Peiwo app**, a stranger voice-social product built around astrological matching and paid calls. It never achieved large-scale success.

## III. TRON and the ICO (2017)

**July 2017** — Founded the **TRON** foundation, positioned as a decentralized content-distribution platform.

**September 2017** — TRON conducted an **ICO (initial coin offering)**, issuing TRX tokens and raising about **$70 million**. According to The Verge, Sun Yuchen rushed to complete the token sale just days before the Chinese government announced its ICO ban, and he was already aware the ban was coming. (Sources: The Verge, Wikipedia)

**Late 2017** — TRX's price surged, briefly entering the top ten by market cap. The whitepaper was widely accused of plagiarizing content from projects including Ethereum and IPFS (Sun Yuchen later called it a "translation issue").

## IV. The BitTorrent acquisition and mainnet launch (2018)

**January 2018** — Signed a letter of intent to acquire BitTorrent; negotiations reportedly began as early as September 2017.

**May 2018** — TRON's mainnet launched, migrating off Ethereum to its own independent blockchain.

**June 2018** — Completed the **$140 million** acquisition of **BitTorrent, Inc.** (later renamed Rainberry Inc.). BitTorrent founder Bram Cohen subsequently left to start a separate crypto project, Chia. (Sources: Variety, Finance Magnates)

**July 2018** — The TRON foundation formally announced the BitTorrent acquisition had closed.

## V. The Buffett lunch incident (2019)

**June 2019** — Won Warren Buffett's charity lunch with a record-setting bid of **$4.567 million**. He heavily promoted it with a public countdown on Twitter and invited several crypto-industry figures, including Litecoin founder Charlie Lee. (Sources: Fortune, CNBC)

**July 22, 2019** — Abruptly announced he was canceling the lunch due to "kidney stones."

**July 23-25, 2019** — Things quickly spiraled. CoinDesk, citing domestic Chinese media, reported that Sun Yuchen had been "barred from leaving the country." He subsequently issued an apology, admitting to excessive hype and immature behavior. (Sources: Washington Post, Bloomberg, CNN)

**January 23, 2020** — The lunch was eventually held as a dinner in Omaha, Nebraska. Attendees included Charlie Lee and eToro CEO Yoni Assia. Buffett later said "cryptocurrency has no value," but had no negative comments about Sun Yuchen personally. (Sources: CoinDesk, CNBC)

## VI. The Steem/Steemit takeover controversy (2020)

**February 2020** — TRON's foundation acquired Steemit from founder Ned Scott for about **$8 million**, gaining roughly **70 million STEEM tokens** (about 20% of total supply).

**February 24, 2020** — Steem's community validator nodes executed a soft fork, freezing Steemit's token voting power.

**Early March 2020** — Sun Yuchen, together with **Binance, Huobi, and Poloniex**, used STEEM tokens held on those exchanges by their users to vote, replacing all 20 top witness nodes in one stroke and completing a "hostile takeover." (Sources: CoinDesk, Decrypt, CoinTelegraph)

**March 20, 2020** — The Steem community hard-forked to create the **Hive** blockchain, and most of the original community migrated over.

## VII. An exchange empire and DeFi/NFTs (2019-2022)

**2019** — Acquired a controlling stake in the crypto exchange **Poloniex**.

**2021** — Appointed by the government of Grenada as **ambassador and permanent representative to the WTO**, gaining the title "His Excellency."

**2021** — Launched DeFi projects in the TRON ecosystem: JustLend, SUN.io, and others. Launched the algorithmic stablecoin **USDD**.

**October 2021** — Participated in a **$65 million funding round** for Animoca Brands.

**2022** — Acquired the **Huobi** exchange (later renamed HTX) through About Capital Management, at a valuation of roughly **$3 billion**. His initial title was "global advisor," though he was the actual controlling party. (Sources: Bloomberg, TechCrunch)

## VIII. The SEC lawsuit and loss of diplomatic status (2023)

**March 2023** — The U.S. SEC sued Sun Yuchen in the Southern District of New York, alleging:
- TRX and BTT tokens were unregistered securities
- Manipulating the TRX market through wash trading, artificially inflating trading volume by **$31 million**
- Paying celebrities to promote the tokens without disclosing the paid relationship
(Sources: SEC official filings, Fortune, CoinDesk)

**March 31, 2023** — The government of Grenada terminated Sun Yuchen's role as WTO ambassador. (Sources: Fortune Crypto, Protos)

**November 2023** — The Poloniex exchange was hacked, with losses exceeding **$125 million**. (Sources: Decrypt, SiliconANGLE)

## IX. Art collecting and his relationship with Trump (2024-2025)

**November 2024** — Won the artwork "Comedian" (the banana artwork) at Sotheby's for **$6.2 million**, then ate it in public. (Sources: Sportskeeda, Slate, Cryptonomist)

**November 2024-January 2025** — Invested a total of roughly **$75 million** in the Trump family's **World Liberty Financial (WLFI)**, becoming its single largest investor. (Sources: Fortune Crypto, CoinDesk)

**April-May 2025** — Bought heavily into the **$TRUMP** memecoin, holding a position worth roughly **$18.6 million**, becoming the top holder on the leaderboard.

**May 22, 2025** — Attended a dinner Trump hosted for top $TRUMP holders. Reportedly received a gold watch as a gift. (Sources: CNBC, CNN, Washington Post)

**September 2025** — WLFI froze $9 million worth of WLFI tokens Sun Yuchen had moved to HTX. Members of Congress sent a letter to the SEC calling for an investigation. (Sources: Fortune, Axios)

## X. The SEC settlement and the latest developments (2026)

**March 5, 2026** — Sun Yuchen reached a **$10 million settlement** with the SEC, admitting no wrongdoing. Widely read as a significant shift in the SEC's enforcement posture toward the crypto industry under the Trump administration. (Sources: US News, CoinDesk, Politico Pro)

**As of April 2026** — the TRON blockchain has cumulatively served over **373 million** users, with over 13 billion total transactions and an on-chain USDT supply exceeding **$85 billion**, making it the largest USDT circulation network in the world. Sun Yuchen's personal net worth is estimated at between **$5 billion and $12.5 billion**. (Sources: Arkham Intelligence, CoinCodex, MEXC)

## Analysis of key turning points

1. **The 2017 ICO**: his transition from a traditional entrepreneur into a crypto player, completing his initial capital accumulation
2. **The 2019 Buffett lunch**: his upgrade from a crypto-world celebrity to a global talking point, but one that also exposed his controversial core
3. **The 2020 Steem incident**: exposed the centralized reality behind the "decentralization evangelist" persona
4. **The 2023 SEC lawsuit**: the most serious legal challenge he's faced, forcing a pivot toward political-relationship investment
5. **Cozying up to Trump in 2024-2025**: the key strategic pivot that let him break out of his legal exposure
