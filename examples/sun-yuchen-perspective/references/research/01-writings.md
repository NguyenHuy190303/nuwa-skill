## I. Formal publications

### 1.1 *This World Is Both Cruel and Gentle* (2017)

- **Type**: autobiography/self-help
- **Publisher**: Jiuzhou Press, March 2017
- **Length**: 288 pages, priced at 39.80 yuan
- **ISBN**: 9787510850660
- **Foreword author**: Feng Lun (founder of Vantone Group)
- **Core narrative**: "over ten years, from a third-tier-university-level score to Peking University, from studying in the US to returning to start a company, from nothing to financial freedom"
- **Chapter themes**: choosing your own dream path, not taking a safe job, independence, investing and entrepreneurship, financial freedom
- **Key quotes**:
  - "this era has fiercer competition, more untamed growth, richer rewards, and more astonishing risk"
  - "the most important thing in life is your framework (geju) — it's like a species' genes, determining from the very source how that species grows"
- **Narrative-packaging analysis**: this book laid the foundation of Sun Yuchen's "grassroots comeback" persona — a student who scored at third-tier-university level going on to Peking University, studying at Penn, achieving financial freedom, a complete class-mobility story
- **Sources**: [Amazon](https://www.amazon.com/这世界既残酷也温柔-孙宇晨/dp/B01LTICE1E) / [Douban](https://book.douban.com/subject/26984629/) / [Goodreads](https://www.goodreads.com/book/show/147050303)
- **Reliability**: primary (his own book)

### 1.2 *Blockchain and the Digital New World* (2023)

- **Type**: industry monograph
- **Publisher**: Contemporary World Press, January 2023
- **Platforms**: Dangdang, Zhangyue app, WeChat Read, etc.
- **Chapter structure** (five chapters):
  1. The arrival of the digital wave
  2. Transformation of the trust mechanism
  3. The iteration of information networks
  4. Reconstruction of the financial network
  5. Reconstruction of the digital world
- **Core argument**: blockchain is the foundational technology of the digital economy — its openness, autonomy, anonymity, and tamper-resistance bring a trust transformation to finance and the internet
- **Narrative-packaging analysis**: an upgrade from "grassroots entrepreneur" to "industry thought leader" persona, attempting to build technical authority
- **Sources**: [WeChat Read](https://weread.qq.com/web/reader/662327d0813ab7d69g01922b) / [ChainCatcher (TechFlow)](https://www.techflowpost.com/newsletter/detail_24922.html) / [Google Books](https://books.google.com/books/about/区块链与数字新世界.html?id=u6Rb0AEACAAJ)

## IV. Technical whitepaper

### 4.1 The TRON whitepaper (2017)

- **Publication date**: 2017
- **Core claim**: building decentralized internet infrastructure
- **The plagiarism controversy (broke out January 2018)**:
  - **Accuser**: Protocol Labs founder Juan Benet pointed out on Twitter that TRON's English-language whitepaper had at least 9 pages of content plagiarized from the IPFS or Filecoin papers
  - **Third-party verification**: BuzzFeed News commissioned a plagiarism analysis via Quetext, which scored the similarity at over 15%, with entire passages of text, diagrams, and phrases identical
  - **Vitalik Buterin's jab**: Ethereum's co-founder mocked the TRON team for boosting whitepaper-writing efficiency with "Ctrl+C + Ctrl+V"
  - **Sun Yuchen's response**: claimed the Chinese-language whitepaper had detailed citations, and that important details were dropped by volunteer translators for the English version
- **Core contradiction**: claims to be a technical innovator, yet the foundational technical document was proven to be extensively plagiarized, and the response dodged the substance
- **Sources**: [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/ryanmac/cryptocurrency-white-paper-plagiarism-tron-justin-sun) / [CoinCentral](https://coincentral.com/community-accuses-tron-plagiarizing-whitepaper/) / [CryptoSlate](https://cryptoslate.com/justin-suns-controversies-plagiarism-teslas-warren-buffett-kidney-stones-and-a-deleted-apology/) / [The Next Web](https://thenextweb.com/hardfork/2018/01/15/cryptocurrency-startup-tron-disaster/)
- **Reliability**: primary (the whitepaper itself) + primary (independent third-party verification)

## XI. How his educational background is packaged

### Key educational milestones

| Time | Credential | Actual situation | How it's packaged |
|------|------|----------|----------|
| ~2011 | Peking University (Bachelor's in History) | Got a bonus-points opportunity through the New Concept essay competition, entering Peking University from a third-tier-university-level score | "From a third-tier score to Peking University" became the core comeback narrative |
| 2011 | Appeared on the cover of *Yazhou Zhoukan* (Asiaweek) | Media exposure began during college | Appeared on the cover twice over 14 years, his "Peking University dream" debut |
| ~2013-2014 | University of Pennsylvania (Master's in East Asian Studies) | Studied in a liberal-arts department, not Wharton Business School | Often vaguely referred to as just "Penn," inviting association with Wharton; he audited classes at Wharton during this time |
| 2014 | Davos Global Shaper | A World Economic Forum program | Used together with the "Jack Ma's disciple" persona to reinforce a global-elite image |
| 2015-2018 | Hupan University | An entrepreneur-training program founded by Jack Ma (not a traditional degree) | "Jack Ma's disciple," "first graduate born in the '90s" used repeatedly |

### Analysis of the packaging pattern

1. **Blurring credentials**: the word "Penn" alone carries a Wharton-adjacent halo, when the actual degree is a Master's in East Asian Studies
2. **Standardizing the comeback narrative**: "third-tier score → Peking University → Penn → financial freedom" became a fixed formula
3. **Stacking celebrity associations**: Peking University + Penn + Davos + Hupan University + Jack Ma + Buffett + Trump — each step links to a bigger name
4. **Claiming labels first**: the "first [90s-born] X" pattern used repeatedly — the first Hupan graduate born in the '90s, China's youngest commercial astronaut, the youngest on Forbes 30 Under 30 Asia, etc.

## XII. Core contradictions

### 12.1 Decentralization vs. centralized control

- **Claimed**: building decentralized internet infrastructure
- **Actual**: personally controls the majority of TRON tokens; nominally an "advisor" to HTX, but multiple legal filings describe him as exercising "effective control" and "common ownership" over entities such as HTX and Poloniex
- **Outside assessment**: "a 'decentralised' network whose economics and governance are in practice dictated by one man and his orbit of entities" — "he is both decentralisation advocate and central planner"
- **Source**: [Medium/Global Times Singapore](https://medium.com/@GlobalTimesSingapore/justin-sun-wants-to-control-the-future-of-crypto-the-question-is-how-much-he-already-does-5ca818c38536)
- **Reliability**: secondary (investigative reporting + legal filings)

### 12.2 Technical innovation vs. whitepaper plagiarism

- **Claimed**: TRON is a world-leading blockchain operating system
- **Actual**: the whitepaper was proven by third-party tools to have extensively plagiarized IPFS and Filecoin (9+ pages, >15% similarity)
- **Response strategy**: blamed the translation volunteers, never directly addressed the plagiarized content itself
- **Source**: [BuzzFeed News](https://www.buzzfeednews.com/article/ryanmac/cryptocurrency-white-paper-plagiarism-tron-justin-sun)
- **Reliability**: primary (independent third-party verification)

### 12.3 Advocating transparency vs. opaque reserves

- **Claimed**: publicly criticized Coinbase's cbBTC for a lack of transparency
- **Actual**: his own Poloniex could not provide proof of reserves ($1.3 billion in BTC unaccounted for); USDD quietly removed $732 million in Bitcoin collateral
- **Sources**: [Protos](https://protos.com/poloniex-and-the-1-3b-bitcoin-question/) / [DL News](https://www.dlnews.com/articles/defi/justin-sun-responds-after-usdd-stablecoin-removes-bitcoin/)

### 12.4 Anti-traditional-finance vs. embracing political power

- **Claimed**: decentralization, trustlessness, code as law
- **Actual**: invested $75 million into the Trump family's crypto project → the SEC paused its lawsuit against him after Trump took office → settled for $10 million
- **Later twist**: in September 2025, World Liberty Financial froze over $100 million of Sun Yuchen's WLFI tokens, and he publicly "pleaded" for them to be unfrozen
- **Sources**: [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2025/02/28/business/crypto-mogul-trump-coins-civil-fraud-charges) / [Fortune](https://fortune.com/crypto/2025/09/05/justin-sun-donald-trump-world-liberty-financial-blacklisted-crypto-address-wlfi/) / [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2025/09/05/trump-justin-sun-world-liberty-financial)
- **Reliability**: primary (SEC filings + on-chain data + public reporting)

### 12.5 Details of the SEC allegations (March 2023 → settled March 2026)

- **Accuser**: the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission (SEC)
- **Defendants**: Sun Yuchen + Tron Foundation Limited + BitTorrent Foundation Ltd. + Rainberry Inc.
- **Core allegations**:
  1. Unregistered securities offerings (TRX and BTT)
  2. Obtained $31 million in illegal proceeds from secondary-market sales of TRX
  3. Directed employees to conduct over 600,000 instances of TRX wash trading between April 2018 and February 2019, moving 4.5 million to 7.4 million TRX per day
  4. Paid celebrities for promotion while requiring them not to disclose the payment (involving 8 celebrities including Lindsay Lohan, Jake Paul, Soulja Boy, Lil Yachty, Ne-Yo, and Akon)
- **Outcome**: in March 2026, under the Trump administration, the SEC terminated the case, and Sun Yuchen's affiliated companies paid a $10 million settlement
- **Sources**: [SEC.gov](https://www.sec.gov/newsroom/press-releases/2023-59) / [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-03-05/sec-ends-case-against-crypto-billionaire-justin-sun) / [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2023/03/22/sec-hits-jake-paul-lindsey-lohan-justin-sun-with-crypto-violations.html)
- **Reliability**: primary (official SEC filings)

## XVII. Research conclusion

Sun Yuchen's public writings and systematic content present a highly self-consistent narrative system, summarized as five progressive layers:

1. **Grassroots underdog** (2017 autobiography) → 2. **Technology evangelist** (whitepaper, monograph) → 3. **Financial-freedom mentor** (Ximalaya, speeches) → 4. **Global elite collector** (hundred-million-dollar-scale art collection) → 5. **Political player** (diplomat, presidential dinner guest)

But this narrative system has multiple structural contradictions: the decentralization ideal vs. centralized control, technical innovation vs. whitepaper plagiarism, advocating transparency vs. opaque reserves, anti-traditional-finance vs. embracing political power.

His core competency isn't technical innovation — it's **manipulating the attention economy**. As "Sun Studies" summarizes it: controversy itself is value; consensus and attention decide everything. The psychological foundation of his methodology is his ability to maintain both his capacity to act and narrative coherence under extreme pressure (an SEC lawsuit, global mockery) — something analysts have called "virtually impossible to transplant."
