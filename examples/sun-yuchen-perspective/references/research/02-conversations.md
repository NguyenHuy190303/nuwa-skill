## V. Key controversial incidents and public responses

### 20. The Buffett-lunch incident (2019-2020)

#### Timeline:
1. **June 2019**: won the Buffett charity lunch for $4.57 million, promoted it loudly
2. **July 2019**: suddenly canceled, citing "a kidney stone attack"
3. **July 2019**: Caixin reported that Sun was detained by Chinese authorities (allegations of illegal fundraising, gambling, money laundering)
4. **July 2019**: Sun posted a long apology on Weibo praising "socialism with Chinese characteristics," then deleted it
5. **January 23, 2020**: the lunch finally took place at a private country club in Omaha

#### Primary sources:
- **The Weibo apology letter** (deleted, screenshots circulated): "I have had the good fortune to grow up alongside the development of socialism with Chinese characteristics... without the regulators' care and guidance, the blockchain industry would not be thriving the way it is today"
  - URL: https://qz.com/1674509/crypto-entrepreneur-justin-sun-apologizes-for-overpromoting-tron
  - **Reliability**: high (multiple outlets preserved screenshots)
- **China Daily English report**: http://global.chinadaily.com.cn/a/201907/25/WS5d397617a310d83056401049.html

#### Secondary sources:
- CoinDesk reporting the lunch finally happening: https://www.coindesk.com/markets/2020/02/06/trons-justin-sun-finally-gets-45m-dinner-with-warren-buffett
- CryptoSlate's five key takeaways: https://cryptoslate.com/justin-sun-lunch-with-warren-buffett/
- Fortune's analysis of the cancellation reasons: https://fortune.com/2019/07/23/warren-buffett-lunch-tron-justin-sun-cryptocurrency/

#### Speech-pattern analysis:
- **Facing Chinese regulators**: immediately switches to "patriotic youth" mode, praises socialism, admits to over-marketing
- **Facing Western media**: sticks to the "kidney stones" story, denies being under investigation
- **After the lunch happened**: repackaged it as "cross-cultural dialogue" rather than self-promotion
- **Key trait**: posted an apology, then deleted it — testing the regulatory temperature, escalating once it seemed insufficient

## VI. Summary of speech patterns

### A. The scenario-switching matrix

| Scenario | Persona | Language style | Attire | Typical line |
|------|------|----------|------|----------|
| Facing Chinese regulators | Patriotic youth | Self-critical, praises socialism | — | "without the regulators' care and guidance..." |
| Facing Western mainstream media (CNBC/Bloomberg) | Mild-mannered tech entrepreneur | Conservative, data-driven | Suit | "We need to learn from traditional finance" |
| Facing industry podcasts | Visionary evangelist | Grand narrative, technical jargon | Casual | "the decentralized internet," "payment rail" |
| Facing policy summits (DC/Milken) | Diplomat/policy-literate figure | Policy language, compliance narrative | Formal | "Building the rails for a unified financial system" |
| Facing the Trump camp | Investor/partner | Mutual business flattery | Formal | "AI+blockchain is a powerful combination" |
| Facing hostile scrutiny | Victim/counter-attacker | Legal threats, denial | — | "blatant defamation," "pursue legal remedies" |
| Facing the community/Twitter | Top whale | Casual, meme-ified | — | emoji, the banana theme, astronaut |

### B. The library of scrutiny-response strategies

1. **Reframing**
   - The Steem takeover → "protecting network security"
   - Accused of wash trading → "too small a share to have had any effect"
   - The banana incident → "conceptual art = blockchain philosophy"

2. **Legal intimidation**
   - Against The Verge: threatened legal action
   - Against Bloomberg: directly sued
   - Against CoinDesk: pressured a retraction

3. **Financial diplomacy**
   - The Buffett lunch: $4.57M buying legitimacy
   - The Grenadian ambassadorship: $150,000-350,000 buying immunity
   - Trump's WLFI: $75M buying political protection
   - The $TRUMP token: becoming the top holder to buy a presidential dinner

4. **Deny & deflect**
   - Kidney stones vs. detained by Chinese authorities
   - "FUD" as the response to an ex-girlfriend's allegations
   - "Neither admit nor deny" for the SEC settlement

5. **Post & delete**
   - The Weibo apology letter: posted → deleted → screenshots already circulated
   - A strategy of testing the water

6. **Counter-attack defense**
   - Facing Vitalik's criticism over Steem → counterattacked with "hope ETC surpasses ETH"
   - Facing the First Digital Trust controversy → counter-accused the other side of "fabricating documents"
   - Facing Bloomberg → sued them for leaking private information

### C. Off-the-cuff analogies and quotable lines

- "The crypto industry is a museum of ideas" (Bitcoin MENA 2024 debate)
- "Our generation's defining traits are resilience, willingness to take risks, and firm conviction" (Artnet NFT interview, 2022)
- Drawing an analogy between conceptual art and blockchain: "most of it exists on the internet rather than in the physical world" (the banana incident, 2024)
- "In the future all crypto exchanges will become decentralized" (Bitcoin.com, 2024)
- "In markets like the US, where financial infrastructure is already strong... blockchain and AI can help expand that system" (DC Summit 2026)

### D. Questions he avoids or refuses to answer

- **The 63% TRX holding**: sued Bloomberg to prevent it from being made public
- **Details of the Chinese-authority investigation**: only ever mentions kidney stones, never directly addresses the Caixin report
- **The FBI investigation**: never responds to The Verge's specific allegations
- **Specific wash-trading data**: deflected through his lawyers' technical arguments
- **The causal link between the WLFI investment and the SEC dropping the case**: never publicly discussed
