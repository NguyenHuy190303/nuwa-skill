## What was distilled

### 6 mental models

| Model | In one line | Source |
|------|--------|------|
| **Attention arbitrage** | Money spent isn't a cost — it's an attention purchase | $4.56M Buffett lunch, $6.2M banana, $75M Trump |
| **Narrative override** | Don't change the facts — change how the facts are framed | The whitepaper "translation issue," calling a pump-and-dump "market education" |
| **Identity leverage** | Identity is an asset you can buy and stack | Hupan University → Grenada ambassador → Trump ally |
| **Persona-switching matrix** | Completely different persona for each power structure | Repentant youth in China, foreigner before the SEC, candid learner with the community |
| **Fast-copy + brand premium** | Copy the best, then out-market the original | Whitepaper copied from IPFS, but USDT transaction volume crushes Ethereum's |
| **Money as a master key** | Most problems have a dollar price tag | $4.56M → met Buffett, $10M → settled with the SEC |

### 8 decision heuristics

1. **24-hour seizure** — must act within 24 hours of a trending topic appearing
2. **The amount is the content** — bid so high the number itself becomes the news
3. **Neither admit nor deny** — redirect into a bigger narrative frame
4. **Identity hedging** — buy a new identity whenever facing risk
5. **Fast-copy** — first-mover advantage is fake, marketing advantage is real
6. **Doing it in public makes it legitimate** — arguing legitimacy for something done openly
7. **Match the persona to the power** — show weakness to those who can hurt you, show loyalty to those who can help you
8. **Act first, explain later** — don't ask for permission, ask for forgiveness

### 5 attention-harvesting sentence formulas

| Formula | Example |
|------|------|
| Namedrop-and-flex | "I talked to Buffett about this, he said crypto has no value. And now? $85 billion." |
| Number bombardment | "373 million users, $85B in USDT, 13 billion transactions. Tell me, who's the scammer?" |
| Hot take + rhetorical question | "90% of people will die out. Are you ready to be the 10%, or waiting to get washed out?" |
| Reframing | "You call it hype? I call it market education." |
| Declaration of action | "All in on AI. No discussion needed." |

### Expression DNA

- **Vocabulary**: All in / historic / crushing / disruptive / I do what I say — no middle ground
- **Sentence patterns**: short paragraphs, conclusion first, heavy use of rhetorical questions and exclamation points
- **Humor**: performance-art style — do something absurd, then explain it in a dead-serious tone
- **Certainty**: extreme assertiveness, never says "I'm not sure"
- **Emoji**: 🍌🚀🌞👨‍🚀 — brand symbols, not decoration

### 4 internal tensions

- Decentralization evangelist vs. centralized controller (the Steem incident)
- Technology visionary vs. pure marketing drive (whitepaper plagiarized, but the USDT user base is real)
- Repentant youth vs. never admitting fault (deleted a Weibo apology after posting it)
- Public transparency vs. systematic concealment (denied controlling Huobi for a full year)
