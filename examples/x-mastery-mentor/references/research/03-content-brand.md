## I. Dan Koe: the One-Person Business and personal-brand framework

### 1.1 Core idea: you are the business itself

Dan Koe's core claim is "turn yourself into a business." Not finding a market and serving it, but productizing your own goals, problems, and path to a solution.

**The Experience Model**: you are your own customer persona. Solve your own problem, attract people like you, systematize the solution — "climb the stairs like everyone else, then lower an elevator down for the people behind you."

> Source (primary): [The One-Person Business Model - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/the-one-person-business-model-how-to-monetize-yourself/)

### 1.2 The four-pillar framework

| Pillar | Definition | Key action |
|------|------|----------|
| **Brand** | your story — what goal you're pursuing, and why | define your vision and anti-vision (what lifestyle you're against) |
| **Content** | your map — guides the audience from point A to point B | 1-3 months of consistent content to build brand awareness |
| **Product** | your solution — start from an MVO (minimum viable offer) | sell first, refine later — start freelancing/consulting at $500-1,000 |
| **Marketing** | your promotion — keep exposing your product | you won't make money without promoting it; beginner-stage content is inherently growth-shaped |

> Source (primary): [The One-Person Business Model - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/the-one-person-business-model-how-to-monetize-yourself/)

### 1.3 The "Niche of One" positioning method: don't find a niche, create one

Dan Koe explicitly rejects traditional niche selection: "you don't need a niche, you need a point of view."

**Three elements that make up your worldview/niche:**

1. **Goals**: self-set goals, not the default ones society hands you (like retiring at 60)
2. **Problems**: the pain points standing in the way of those goals
3. **Potential Paths**: a clear, step-by-step system

**The Interest Stack method:**
- Don't position around a single field — combine multiple interests (fitness + philosophy + business + lifestyle)
- The intersection of interests produces a unique perspective and prevents commodity-style competition
- A narrow niche grows ~5,000 followers a year; a wide niche + a unique combination can grow ~20,000 a year

**The core formula: "a wide brand, a narrow product"**
- At the content level: share philosophy, education, and practice across your diverse interests (don't sell directly)
- At the product level: build a static landing page targeting a specific problem — the product is narrower than the content

> "You aren't targeting a niche. You are a niche and your job is to persuade people to join it."

> Source (primary): [How To Create Your Niche Of One - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/how-to-create-your-niche-of-one-become-nicheless/)
> Source (primary): [You don't need a niche, you need a point of view - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.medium.com/you-dont-need-a-niche-you-need-a-point-of-view-0a22b5e2802b)

### 1.4 The "Book of Your Life" brand structure

Think of your brand as a book with four chapters:

| Chapter | Content | Suggested share |
|------|------|----------|
| **Introduction** | your origin story and transformation narrative | — |
| **Philosophy** | your answer to "what is a good life," defining the enemy and your extreme beliefs | 40% |
| **Education** | teaching the skills and knowledge within your brand's scope | 40% |
| **Practice** | creating step-by-step systems and named frameworks (like "The 2 Hour Writer") | 20% |

> Source (secondary, a distillation of Dan Koe's methodology): [A distilled knowledge base for building a personal brand - Dan Koe method](https://digital-garden.ontheagilepath.net/a-distilled-knowledge-base-for-building-a-personal-brand-base-on-dan-koes-method)

### 1.5 The content-creation engine: the 2 Hour Writer system

**Core process: 1 hour walking to find inspiration + 1 hour writing and editing**

**A 6-step creative-thinking process:**
1. Pick an idea from a book, podcast, or social media
2. Ask 3+ questions about that idea ("why?" "how?")
3. Write a first draft without judging it, using the PAS framework (Problem-Amplify-Solution)
4. Review it from the reader's perspective — a scrolling phone user needs to be entertained
5. Anticipate objections, leave room for debate
6. Format: add line breaks, polish the hook, cut filler

**The Idea Museum**:
- Maintain an organized library of material, prioritizing sources like old/obscure books, curated blogs, and high-quality accounts
- Pick topics at the intersection of "Performance" (could go viral) and "Excitement" (you're interested in it)

**A quick-reference writing framework:**
- **Listicle**: hook > list of points > conclusion
- **A short post (1-2 sentences)**: a personal redefinition / a "hard truth" / "if this, then that"
- **The P-S-B story arc**: Pain point → Solution → Benefit

> Source (primary): [The 2 Hour Writer](https://2hourwriter.com/)
> Source (secondary): [Dan Koe's 2-Hour Writer summary](https://ilyaas.substack.com/p/dan-koes-2-hour-writer)

### 1.6 The content-repurposing flywheel

**Core principle: everything starts with the newsletter — 30 minutes a day writing the newsletter, everything else takes 10 minutes.**

**The specific process:**

```
Newsletter (long-form, 1-2 issues per week)
    ├── extract 5-7 short posts → Twitter/X
    ├── screenshot Twitter posts → Instagram / LinkedIn
    ├── read the newsletter aloud → YouTube video
    ├── YouTube audio → podcast platforms
    └── a high-engagement tweet → expands into next issue's newsletter topic
```

**Weekly schedule:**
- Sunday: newsletter outline (30-60 min)
- Monday-Friday: write one newsletter section daily (30-60 min) + schedule daily posts (30 min) + engagement/promotion (30 min)
- Saturday: publish the newsletter + write a promotional post

**The key strategy: Twitter tests first.** Its character limit forces you to distill the core idea. Once an idea works on Twitter, expand it into the newsletter, and the newsletter becomes a YouTube script.

> Source (primary): [Dan Koe Twitter](https://x.com/thedankoe)
> Source (secondary): [Dan Koe's Newsletter Repurposing Content System](https://mikeromaine.com/dan-koes-newsletter-repurposing-content-system/)

### 1.7 Specific Twitter growth strategy

**At the writing level:**
- The hook is the heart of everything — spend as much time on the hook as writing the rest of the content
- Followers don't want "more content," they want a solution to their problem
- A "sharp opinion" is what makes you stand out from everyone saying the same thing

**At the engagement/growth level:**
- **The DJ method**: create a thread curating other people's top posts (e.g. "most people are terrible at X... here are 7 of the best posts on it, done in 5 minutes"), tag the original authors. They'll retweet it, and you gain access to their followers
- **Tribe injection**: reply daily to target accounts' content -> move the conversation from replies to DMs -> ask how they're growing -> propose mutual support
- **High-quality replies**: don't write "great post" — write a 200-400-word "mini-newsletter-style reply"
- **Long content builds trust**: short content gets you exposure, long content (newsletters, articles) builds deep trust and authority

**A 7-step, non-begging way to build a DM network:**
1. Find people genuinely aligned with your goals
2. Give a specific compliment (about their work)
3. Ask about their goals/projects
4. Offer value first (share resources, make an introduction)
5. Optional: move to a call or a deeper conversation
6. Follow up with relevant resources
7. Only make an ask once a solid relationship exists

> Source (primary): [The Best Way To Grow On Social Media - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/the-best-way-to-grow-on-social-media-from-zero-followers-experience/)
> Source (primary): [How To Actually Grow On Social Media - Dan Koe](https://thedankoe.com/letters/how-to-actually-grow-on-social-media-what-they-dont-tell-you/)

## II. Alex Hormozi: value-first and the content machine

### 2.1 Core philosophy: "Give Away the Secrets, Sell the Implementation"

Hormozi's content philosophy in one line: **give away the secret for free, sell the execution.**

- Only 1% of people will go do it themselves; 99% would rather pay someone else to do it for them
- Give away high-value content for free -> prove you have the solution -> build trust and reciprocity -> conversion follows naturally
- He's executed this strategy since 2022, spending zero dollars on ads, and gained over 1 million followers in 6 months

**Follower counts as of August 2025:**
- YouTube: 3.55 million
- Instagram: 4 million
- X/Twitter: 918,000
- LinkedIn: 714,000

> Source (primary): [Alex Hormozi on X - "Giveaway the secrets, sell the implementation"](https://x.com/AlexHormozi/status/1529553072136998914)
> Source (secondary): [Content Repurposing Strategy: 180k to 1.2M Followers - Copyblogger](https://copyblogger.com/content-repurposing/)

### 2.2 The Hook-Retain-Reward framework

This is the core three-part structure behind Hormozi's content strategy:

**1. HOOK — instantly seize attention**

| Technique type | Example |
|----------|------|
| Shock/the unexpected | drop a counterintuitive fact or statistic |
| A provocative question | trigger curiosity with a question |
| A bold promise | a clear, achievable benefit |

> Example: "Most people are wasting 80% of their marketing budget — and they don't even know it."

**2. RETAIN — keep delivering value**
- Keep it compelling with story + open loops (suspense)
- Every sentence or frame must deliver value, zero filler
- Break up the rhythm with headlines, bullet points, and visual elements
- Keep referencing the audience's pain points

**3. REWARD — over-deliver**
- Give actionable advice the audience can act on immediately
- Deliver more value than promised (promise 1 bonus, give 3)
- Provide free resources with zero friction
- Leave the audience looking forward to the next piece of content

> Source (secondary, a systematic analysis of Hormozi's strategy): [Alex Hormozi's Content Strategy: Hook, Retain, and Reward Explained](https://itsmostly.com/blog/alex-hormozis-content-strategy-hook-retain-and-reward-explained)

### 2.3 The Value Equation: writing an irresistible hook

**Formula: Value = (Dream Outcome × Perceived Likelihood) / (Time Delay × Effort & Sacrifice)**

The bigger the numerator and the smaller the denominator, the more irresistible the hook.

| Variable | Role | How to strengthen it |
|------|------|----------|
| **Dream Outcome** | the best result the reader could get | paint a vivid picture of the best-case scenario |
| **Perceived Likelihood** | how much the reader believes they can achieve it | use "step-by-step," "proven system," "fail-proof" |
| **Time Delay** | how fast they'll see results | use a short timeframe (10 days vs. 3 months), but keep it realistic |
| **Effort & Sacrifice** | how much effort is required | use "simple steps," "easy-to-follow," "beginner-friendly" |

**In practice: before writing a hook, systematically assess all four variables:**
1. Can I amplify the dream outcome and the sense of it being achievable?
2. Can I shorten the timeline and lower the required effort?

> Source (secondary): [How to Use Alex Hormozi's Value Equation to Write Viral Hooks](https://davidschwertfeger.com/newsletter/alex-hormozis-value-equation-to-write-viral-hooks/)
> Source (primary, a book): Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers*

### 2.4 The MAGIC headline formula

From *$100M Offers*, originally for naming products/offers, but also usable for content headlines:

| Letter | Meaning | Explanation |
|------|------|------|
| **M** | Magnet (a reason to pay attention) | give a reason to care, like "back-to-school discount," "limited time" |
| **A** | Avatar (customer persona) | explicitly call out your target audience, the more specific the better |
| **G** | Goal | what the customer is chasing: pain-free, doubled profit, first customer |
| **I** | Interval (the timeframe) | how long until it's achieved? "pain-free in 10 days" |
| **C** | Container word | signals this is a packaged offering: Challenge, Blueprint, Bootcamp |

**Key guidance: you don't need to use all 5 letters — 2-3 is enough. Shorter names are better, more specific is better.**

> Source (primary, a book): Alex Hormozi, *$100M Offers*
> Source (secondary): [Alex Hormozi's MAGIC Headline Copywriting Formula - Breakthrough Marketing Secrets](https://www.breakthroughmarketingsecrets.com/blog/alex-hormozis-magic-headline-copywriting-formula-from-the-100m-offers-book/)

### 2.5 Hook strategy based on audience-awareness stage

Hormozi adjusts the hook type based on how aware the audience already is:

| Awareness stage | Hook type | Example direction |
|----------|----------|----------|
| **Unaware** | curiosity-driven hooks | makes them think "what is this?" |
| **Problem-Aware** | problem-focused hooks | highlights the pain point and the solution |
| **Solution-Aware** | solution-comparison hooks | why your solution is better |
| **Product-Aware** | competitor-comparison hooks | a direct comparison against competitors |

> Source (secondary): [How I Write Hooks That Actually Work - Alex Hormozi's Proven Method](https://solopreneurcode.substack.com/p/how-i-write-hooks-that-actually-work)

### 2.6 Twitter as a content testing ground

**Hormozi treats Twitter as "the lowest-cost platform for validating ideas":**

1. Post 5 tweets a day testing different ideas
2. Watch which ideas get the most engagement
3. High-engagement tweets -> expand into long-form video content
4. Long-form video -> cut into 30+ short clips distributed to Reels/TikTok/Shorts

**Content style characteristics:**
- Concise, direct, quotable-line-style business advice
- Short parallel sentence structures, with a longer final sentence that summarizes
- Tweets about making money perform best, especially contrasting rich vs. poor mindsets
- Highest-posting day is Tuesday

> Source (primary): [Alex Hormozi X Profile](https://x.com/AlexHormozi)
> Source (secondary): [Who's Alex Hormozi? - Favikon](https://www.favikon.com/blog/whos-alex-hormozi)

### 2.7 The content-repurposing machine: from 7 to 80 pieces a week

**Growth data (over 6 months):**
- YouTube: 70K -> 300K
- Twitter: 10K -> 100K
- Instagram: 70K -> 330K
- SEO traffic: 0 -> 22K/month
- Podcast downloads: 20K -> 150K/month

**A 5-person repurposing team structure:**

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|------|
| Twitter Editor | Twitter content optimization and scheduling |
| YouTube Editor | long-form YouTube video editing |
| LinkedIn Editor | adapting content for LinkedIn |
| Podcast Editor | podcast production and distribution |
| Reels/TikTok Editor | short-video clipping and optimization |

**Total cost: $40,000/month**

**A four-step workflow:**
1. **Validate**: test ideas cheaply on Twitter
2. **Create**: produce video as the primary format (because video can be broken down into audio + text + images, but not the reverse)
3. **Edit & Contextualize**: the team clips it per platform, inserting CTAs at the start/middle/end
4. **Distribute & Monitor**: publish on a fixed schedule using tools like Buffer

> Source (secondary): [Content Repurposing Strategy: 180k to 1.2M Followers - Copyblogger](https://copyblogger.com/content-repurposing/)

## IV. Extracting an actionable framework (applicable to the persona's X strategy)

### Framework 1: a three-step brand-positioning method (from Dan Koe)

1. **Define your vision and anti-vision**: what kind of life are you pursuing? What are you against?
2. **List your Interest Stack**: AI programming + indie hacking + content creation + personal growth -> the intersection produces a unique perspective
3. **Write "The Book of Your Life"**: origin story + philosophical stance + educational content + a practice system

### Framework 2: the content-creation flywheel (combining both people's methods)

```
Twitter tweets (3-5 a day, testing ideas)
    │
    ├── high-engagement tweets → a long-form newsletter/blog piece
    │                    │
    │                    ├── a YouTube video script
    │                    └── an image-and-text post for other platforms
    │
    └── low-engagement tweets → rewrite from a different angle, or drop it
```

### Framework 3: a hook-writing checklist (from Hormozi)

Before writing any hook, ask yourself:
- [ ] Is the Dream Outcome clear? Can the reader picture the best-case result?
- [ ] Is it credible enough? Is it backed by data/case studies/a system?
- [ ] Is the timeline specific? Shorter is more compelling
- [ ] Is the barrier low? Does the reader feel they can actually do it?

### Framework 4: an authority-building path

| Stage | Strategy | Source |
|------|------|------|
| 0-1K followers | the DJ method (curating others' content) + tribe injection (high-quality replies) | Dan Koe |
| 1K-10K followers | daily tweets + a weekly newsletter + sharp opinions | Dan Koe + Hormozi |
| 10K-100K followers | a content-repurposing machine + video expansion + free high-value resources | Hormozi |
| 100K+ followers | named frameworks (like "The 2 Hour Writer") + a product ecosystem | both |
